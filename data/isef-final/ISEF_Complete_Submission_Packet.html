1. Official 250-Word ISEF Abstract

Title: BolMizan: An Offline-First, Cryptographically Tamper-Evident Ledger and Graph-Based Circular Debt Clearing System with Multilingual Anti-Scam Detection for Low-Resource Merchants

Category: Systems Software (SOFT)

Small-scale retail merchants in semi-urban and rural Pakistan rely on handwritten paper ledgers (kacha khata), leaving them vulnerable to record tampering, internet outages, circular supply-chain illiquidity, and regional-language SMS payment scams. This engineering project presents BolMizan, an offline-first, bank-grade bookkeeping and fraud-detection system supporting Sindhi, Urdu, Roman Urdu, and English.

The architecture integrates four core modules: (1) an asynchronous IndexedDB and SQLite WAL ledger utilizing SHA-256 hash-chaining, Merkle root verification, and Lamport logical clocks; (2) a zero-loss integer-paisa double-entry accounting engine with an alternative merchant trust scoring model (300–850 scale); (3) Mukasa, a graph-based net-balance solver that resolves circular debt cycles without physical cash; and (4) a deterministic edge anti-scam classifier.

Testing on a 300-sample multilingual dataset achieved 90.00% classification accuracy and an 88.89% F1-Score at an average latency of 0.0479 milliseconds, while the Mukasa solver resolved circular debts across 1,000 simulated merchant nodes in under 5 milliseconds.
